1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

High-security environments in the financial sector—specifically 24/7 standalone ATM kiosks, bank branches, and currency transfer vaults—face acute physical vulnerability. Current surveillance reliance on passive video recording (DVR/NVR) or remote human security operators exhibits critical systemic flaws:

  • Operational Latency: Human response times to physical robberies or aggressive coercion exceed 4 to 8 minutes, rendering CCTV footage solely forensic rather than preventative.
  • High False Alarm Rates: Basic motion-triggered sensors trip indiscriminately on harmless actions (sweeping, customer gestures, door vibrations), leading to alert fatigue.
  • Inference Bottlenecks: Conventional AI surveillance pipelines couple heavy deep neural networks directly into the video display thread, introducing catastrophic 2–4 second video lag and buffer overruns.
Core Problem: Financial institutions lack an affordable, edge-compatible solution capable of continuously isolating human altercations in real time without lag, while filtering false positives from variable lighting and ambient clutter.

2. The Proposed Solution & AI/ML Architecture

Sentinel AI is an end-to-end edge surveillance engine engineered to deliver zero-latency threat detection across live CCTV feeds, RTSP streams, and mobile IP cameras. The framework resolves the traditional throughput bottleneck via a Decoupled Dual-Thread Architecture:

Thread 1: Dedicated Frame & Streaming Loop

Operates an asynchronous capture worker feeding lockless queues. Handles MJPEG client encoding independently at 30–60 FPS, ensuring zero stutter for monitoring personnel.

Thread 2: Asynchronous AI Inference Engine

Consumes downsampled frames at a fixed 15 Hz cadence, executing multi-stage spatial tracking, dynamic noise estimation, and 3D CNN kinetic action evaluation.

3. Deep Learning & Mathematical Pipeline

The intelligence layer operates through a three-stage mathematical pipeline:

  • Layer 1: Adaptive Illumination Equalization: Deploys CIE-LAB color space transformation with Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) on the L-channel (clip limit 2.0, 8x8 grid). This neutralizes harsh shadows and glaring backlights typical of glass ATM vestibules.
  • Layer 2: Spatial Multi-Person Proximity Tracking: Uses Ultralytics YOLO11n combined with ByteTrack. Calculates Euclidean pairwise centroid distances and bounding box IoU overlaps to identify clustered human interactions and isolate altercations.
  • Layer 3: 3D Spatio-Temporal Action Modeling: Maintains a 16-frame rolling temporal action window spanning exactly 1.0 second. Feeds a lightweight X3D-M 3D convolutional network pre-trained on kinetic human interactions to extract temporal aggression signatures.
  • Threat Memory Integration: Predictions are processed through an Exponential Leaky Integrator ($\gamma = 0.88$) and dynamic ambient $z$-score thresholding ($Z = \frac{E - \mu_{ambient}}{\sigma_{ambient}}$). Solo aggression triggers at threshold 0.42, while multi-person scuffles trip at 0.35.

5. Business Impact & FinTech Relevance

ATM Vestibule & Branch Safety

Provides unattended 24/7 protection for isolated ATMs and cash deposits, alerting central security hubs within 150 milliseconds of physical violence or coercion.

Cost Reduction & Scalability

Operates entirely on commodity consumer hardware or existing NVR boxes without requiring multi-thousand dollar proprietary hardware upgrades.

Forensic Snapshot Vault

Automatically archives timestamped high-resolution evidence snapshots upon threat confirmation, safeguarding evidentiary integrity for law enforcement.

Zero Privacy Intrusions

Executes locally on the edge. No biometric identity databases, face recognition, or PII retention are utilized, ensuring strict regulatory compliance.
